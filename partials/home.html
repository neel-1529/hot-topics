Article 1 – The Value of Money

Money plays a fundamental role in our daily lives, acting as a medium of exchange, a unit of account, and a store of value. It allows individuals and businesses to trade goods and services efficiently without relying on barter systems. The value of money, however, is not fixed—it changes over time due to inflation, economic policies, and market demand.

One of the most important aspects of money is how people perceive its value. For some, money represents security and stability, while for others it symbolizes power and success. Financial literacy is essential in understanding how to manage money effectively, including saving, investing, and budgeting. Without proper knowledge, individuals may struggle to make informed financial decisions.

In today’s global economy, digital transactions and online banking have transformed how we use money. Cash is gradually being replaced by electronic payments, making transactions faster and more convenient. However, this also raises concerns about cybersecurity and financial privacy. Understanding the value of money is not just about earning it, but also about using it wisely to achieve long-term goals.

Article 2 – GMO Food

Genetically Modified Organisms (GMOs) are organisms whose genetic material has been altered using biotechnology. In agriculture, GMO crops are designed to improve yield, resist pests, and tolerate harsh environmental conditions. This innovation has helped farmers increase productivity and reduce the use of chemical pesticides.

Despite their advantages, GMO foods remain a topic of debate. Supporters argue that GMOs can help solve global food shortages and improve nutritional value. On the other hand, critics raise concerns about potential health risks and environmental impact. The long-term effects of consuming GMO foods are still being studied, making it a controversial issue worldwide.

Governments and organizations regulate GMO production to ensure safety and transparency. Labels on food products often indicate whether they contain genetically modified ingredients, allowing consumers to make informed choices. As technology advances, the role of GMOs in food production will likely continue to evolve.

Article 3 – The World in 2100

The world in 2100 is expected to be shaped by rapid technological advancements, environmental changes, and evolving societies. Artificial intelligence, robotics, and automation may dominate industries, transforming how people work and live. Smart cities could become the norm, integrating technology into everyday life to improve efficiency and sustainability.

Climate change will likely play a significant role in shaping the future. Rising sea levels, extreme weather conditions, and resource scarcity may challenge global stability. Governments and organizations are already working toward sustainable solutions, such as renewable energy and eco-friendly infrastructure, to address these issues.

By 2100, human life may also extend beyond Earth, with space exploration and colonization becoming more realistic. Advances in medicine and genetics could increase life expectancy and improve quality of life. While the future holds many uncertainties, it also offers opportunities for innovation and progress that could redefine humanity.
